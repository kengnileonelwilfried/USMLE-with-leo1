The Exams

Tutoring built around what each Step actually tests.

Step 1, Step 2 CK, and Step 3 reward different skills. The plan changes accordingly.

Step 1 — Foundations

Pass/fail, but the mechanisms you build here carry you through the rest of your career. The focus is depth, retention, and comfort with the reasoning — not speed-running content.

  • High-yield basic science, mapped to First Aid
  • Active recall and spaced repetition built into the schedule
  • Strong foundations in pathophysiology and pharmacology
  • NBME-style practice woven in from week one
FORMATPass / Fail
TYPICAL PREP WINDOW12–20 weeks
PRIMARY TOOLSFirst Aid, UWorld, AMBOSS
SESSION FOCUSMechanism-first review
CHECKPOINTSWeekly NBME-style blocks

Step 2 CK — Clinical Reasoning

This exam rewards clinical judgment, not memorization. Sessions shift toward case-based reasoning, next-best-step thinking, and the guidelines that actually show up on shelf exams.

  • Clinical reasoning and CCS-style case approach
  • High-yield topics mapped to current clinical guidelines
  • UWorld and CMS-style practice, question by question
  • Integration with shelf exams during clinical rotations
FORMAT3-digit score
TYPICAL PREP WINDOW8–16 weeks
PRIMARY TOOLSUWorld, NBME, CMS cases
SESSION FOCUSCase-based decision making
CHECKPOINTSTimed NBME assessments

Step 3 — Independent Practice

Often squeezed into residency, with the least time and the highest stakes for licensure. Sessions are built for efficiency — targeted review, CCS-case fluency, and a schedule that respects your call rotation.

  • Efficient, residency-compatible session scheduling
  • CCS case simulation and management-focused practice
  • Targeted review of foundational medicine and biostatistics
  • Final-stretch mock exams under real time pressure
FORMAT2-day, MCQ + CCS
TYPICAL PREP WINDOW6–12 weeks
PRIMARY TOOLSUWorld CCS, NBME forms
SESSION FOCUSManagement & disposition
CHECKPOINTSFull-length timed simulations
